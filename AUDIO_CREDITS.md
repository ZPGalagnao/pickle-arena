# Audio credits

Pickle Arena uses these free recordings from [OpenGameArt.org](https://opengameart.org).
They were trimmed, mixed, re-levelled and converted to OGG by `tools/make_audio.py`.
The other sounds (special attacks, footsteps, net, UI, slow motion) are synthesized
by `tools/make_sounds.py`.

| Menu music (`music_menu.ogg`, first 2:30) | [Well oiled, shiny Oscillators](https://opengameart.org/content/well-oiled-shiny-oscillators) | Viktor Kraus | [CC-BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) |
